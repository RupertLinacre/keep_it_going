# Christmas-world aesthetic exploration

Branch: `codex/christmas-world`
Date: 5 October 2026
Generation method: built-in `image_gen` tool, with existing gameplay images as visual references.

These are generated design concepts for a possible fifth world, not actual
screenshots of an implemented Christmas level. The game itself remains unchanged.
Each aesthetic has two scenes: A emphasizes the setting around a modest track
feature; B explores a signature looping or spiralling attraction in that setting.

## Shared visual rules

The camera is an orthographic, slightly elevated near-side view: approximately
12 degrees of yaw and 24 degrees of downward pitch. It is not a top-down
isometric miniature. The track extends beyond the image edges, with thin double
rails, repeated sleepers, slender supports and clear uninterrupted silhouettes.
The train remains six small angular pastel coaches, several with two round eyes,
alternating with open parcel wagons, and a small white steam plume at the engine.

Landscape is layered, visibly faceted and built from broad colour areas. Props
combine boxes, cones, low-sided cylinders, planes and polygonal rocks. Materials
are mostly matte and untextured. Shadows are simple and directional; night-world
lighting adds restrained emissive bulbs and small halos. Snow should be solid
geometric caps and drifts, and water broad flat shapes. Details are concentrated
in small groups, with clear space around the rails and train. Characters are
small toys, not realistic humans. No photographic surfaces, dense forests,
cinematic fog, volumetric light, glossy candy or heavy particle effects.

A generated concept is not a performance benchmark. Plausible implementation
means repeating and batching simple geometry, limiting independent lights,
keeping effects bounded and retaining the existing terrain and camera approach.
