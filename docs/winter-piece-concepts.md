# Winter special-piece concepts

40 generated proposals on `codex/christmas-world`, for selection before implementation.
01–20 belong to Twilight Lapland; 21–40 to Frosty Lake Fair.

Review gallery: <http://localhost:5198/output/imagegen/christmas-pieces/index.html>.
Images and the portable gallery are in `output/imagegen/christmas-pieces/`.
Full descriptions and built-in imagegen prompts are saved in
`design/christmas-aesthetics/special-pieces.json` and the gallery's `prompts.json`.
Generation provenance is saved beside the images in `generation-records.json`.

Use the world filter, click an image to enlarge it, tick favourites and copy
their unique numbers. Selections are stored locally in the browser; nothing is sent.

The concepts emphasise track/scenery relationships and readable train clearance:
rising and falling wraps, open arches, rooftop humps, switchbacks, loops through
handles, dry tunnels and large playful mechanisms reacting to the passing train.
These are inspiration images, not geometry or performance claims about implemented pieces.
